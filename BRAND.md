# SYNXO — Nano AI Agent

## Brand identity

**Name:** SYNXO
**Product:** Nano AI Agent
**Tagline:** Builds. Learns. Deploys. Automatically.

SYNXO is designed as a compact agent focused on building, evaluating, versioning, and deploying small AI models with minimal compute and token overhead.

## Logo concept

The mark combines three ideas:

- **AI head:** the dark rounded form represents a compact intelligent core.
- **Green eyes:** focused inference and machine perception.
- **Growing leaves:** controlled learning and model improvement.
- **Flowing outer stroke:** an autonomous build/deploy loop.
- **SYNXO wordmark:** a technical, concise identity suitable for a developer product.

## Primary visual language

- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Core charcoal: `#172027`
- Secondary gray: `#5A646B`
- Accent green: `#39D98A`
- Supporting green: `#1E8F67`
- Style: clean, flat, geometric, modern, minimal

## Product principles

1. Small models first.
2. Prefer parameter-efficient training such as LoRA/PEFT where appropriate.
3. Evaluate every candidate model before promotion.
4. Keep model versions reproducible.
5. Deploy only validated versions.
6. Minimize unnecessary inference and training tokens.
7. Keep the model-building subsystem separate from general agent orchestration.
